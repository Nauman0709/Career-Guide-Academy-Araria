Admissions & Fee Policy

Transparent, merit-guided admissions for Session 2026–27. Complete verified schedule of admission fees, tuition rates, and local conveyance charges.

Enrollment Flow

Step-by-Step Admission Process

A straightforward four-stage pathway designed for families seeking excellence in education.

1

Initial Enquiry

Submit the online enquiry form below or collect the admission proforma in person from the school reception at Rampur Kodarkatti.

2

Academic Interaction

Candidate participates in an age-appropriate scholastic evaluation (English, Mathematics, Science/General Knowledge) and personal interaction.

3

Document Verification

Submit original Transfer Certificate (TC), municipal birth certificate, past mark sheets, and photographs to the administrative office.

4

Enrollment & House

Payment of verified registration and admission fees, issuance of student ID, textbook kits, school uniform, and house allotment.

Prerequisites

Eligibility Criteria

Admissions are conducted strictly in accordance with CBSE norms and Right to Education provisions without discrimination regarding religion, caste, creed, or gender:

  • • Primary (Class 1 to 5): Minimum age of 6 completed years for Class 1 by March 31 of the academic year. Satisfactory prior foundational readiness.
  • • Middle (Class 6 to 8): Successful completion and promotion from the preceding grade in a recognized institution.
  • • Secondary (Class 9): Valid pass certificate in Class VIII from a CBSE or recognized state/central board with authentic Transfer Certificate.
  • • Class 10 Admissions: Strictly governed by CBSE transfer guidelines (relocation of parents or transfer from another CBSE affiliated school).

Mandatory Documentation Checklist

  • Transfer Certificate (TC): Original counter-signed TC from the previous recognized school (view sample TC format).
  • Birth Certificate: Self-attested copy of Birth Certificate issued by Municipal Corporation or Gram Panchayat.
  • Previous Academic Report Card: Copy of the annual evaluation marksheet of the qualifying standard.
  • Identity Proof: Photocopies of Student and Parent/Guardian Aadhaar Cards.
  • Photographs: 4 recent passport-size colour photographs of candidate and 2 each of parents.
