Academics & Curriculum

Rigorous CBSE-aligned pedagogy, verified Class X subject combinations, continuous formative evaluation, and vibrant co-scholastic learning.

Academic Foundation

Continuous Evaluation & Mentorship

Under the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE Affiliation No: 330606), Career Guide Academy organizes academic instruction to cultivate independent inquiry, logical deduction, and sound linguistic articulation.

With a 1.5 : 1 teacher-to-section ratio and a dedicated faculty of 25 qualified educators (PGTs, TGTs, PRTs), student progress is monitored through day-to-day assessments, two comprehensive terminal examinations, and structured diagnostic feedback.

Specialized Student Support

The school employs a certified Special Educator (Shahzad Alam) and a dedicated Counsellor & Student Wellness Teacher (Anjali Kumari) to provide targeted academic remediation and social-emotional guidance.

Academic Rules & Promotion Policy

  • • Academic Session: Commences in April and concludes on March 31 annually. Regular class instruction begins the first week of April.
  • • Terminal Examinations: Two terminal examinations are conducted yearly. A minimum score of 60% in terminal examinations is required for standard promotion.
  • • Mandatory Attendance: Students must attend a minimum of 85% of total delivered classes to be eligible for annual promotion examinations.
  • • Vacation Schedule: Summer vacation runs from mid-June to early July, balanced with gazetted national and cultural holidays.

Co-Scholastic Life

The Four-House Community System

Every enrolled student is inducted into one of four houses, cultivating mutual respect, teamwork, leadership, and athletic competition.

Q

Quayam House

Red T-Shirt

Honors Quayam Educational and Welfare Trust, upholding institutional stewardship, service, and benevolence in all endeavours.

R

Rahman House

Blue T-Shirt

Embodies scientific inquiry, calmness, perseverance, and ethical dedication to scholarly pursuits.

S

S.R.K House

Green T-Shirt

Represents vitality, communal brotherhood, cultural creativity, and harmony across diverse communities.

A

A.P.J.K House

White T-Shirt

Named after Bharat Ratna Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam, inspiring scientific ambition, humility, and patriotism.

Dress Code & Dignity

Official Academic Uniform Guidelines

Promoting equality, neatness, and institutional pride. All students must wear the designated dress code in clean condition daily.

Boys Regular Uniform

  • Shirt: Sky Blue Small Check Shirt
  • Trousers: Carbon Blue Pant
  • Winter Wear: Maroon Blazer and Sweater
  • Accessories: Official School Tie and School Belt
  • Footwear: Black Shoes with White Socks

Girls Regular Uniform

  • Top: Sky Blue Small Check Shirt or Kurti with Carbon Blue Belt
  • Bottom: Carbon Blue Skirt or White Salwar
  • Winter Wear: Maroon Blazer and Sweater
  • Accessories: Official School Tie and School Belt
  • Footwear: Black Shoes with White Socks

House Days (Wed & Sat)

  • Top: Respective House T-Shirt (Red, Blue, Green, or White)
  • Boys Bottom: Clean White Pant
  • Girls Bottom: Clean White Skirt / Salwar
  • Footwear: White Canvas Shoes with White Socks
  • Note: Worn on all sports and inter-house competition days
