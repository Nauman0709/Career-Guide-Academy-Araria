Key Learning Spaces

Verified Facilities Breakdown

Designed to foster intellectual rigor, experimental discovery, physical vitality, and student health.

Science Laboratories (Physics, Chemistry & Biology)

Four dedicated science laboratories totaling 600 sq. meters equipped with apparatus, optical benches, chemical reagents, and compound microscopes enabling Class IX and X students to master CBSE practical curricula first-hand.

Capacity: 4 Subject-Specific Laboratories

ICT Computer Laboratory

Equipped with 40 computer workstations linked via high-speed local networking and web server infrastructure. Students receive hands-on instruction in software literacy, algorithmic logic, and academic digital tools.

Capacity: 40 Networked Workstations • Wi-Fi Enabled

Institutional Library & Reading Hall

Spacious 30×20 ft reading hall managed by qualified librarian Md. Rizwanul Hoda, housing 3,000+ curriculum reference volumes, 18 national periodicals, 25 magazines, and 7 daily newspapers.

Holdings: 3,000+ Reference Books • 18 Periodicals

Campus Health Clinic & First Aid

Dedicated on-campus infirmary fitted with 5 patient beds, essential emergency medications, first-aid resources, and visiting panel doctors & nurses. Periodic medical health check-ups ensure student well-being.

Facilities: 5 Clinic Beds • 2 Panel Doctors • 3 Nurses

Athletic Grounds & Indoor Games

2,024 sq. meter open-air playground for outdoor cricket, football, and athletics drills, complemented by an indoor activity room for table tennis, carrom, chess, and Bharat Scouts & Guides parade drills.

Area: 2,024 m² Playground • 2 Physical Training Instructors

School Bus Fleet & Logistics

Dedicated school-owned bus fleet operated by 3 verified drivers and supervised by Transport Coordinator Shankar Kumar Yadav (9162430014). Connects 8 local panchayats safely to campus.

Fleet: 2 School Buses • Speed Governors • Female Escort
