Institutional Heritage

Education as We Perceive: Nurturing Character Since 1996

Career Guide Academy was founded in 1996 by eminent scholar Prof. Raqueeb Ahmad with the distinguished support of Md. Iqubal (Alig), Abrar Ahmad, and Syed Shamim Anwar, under the aegis of Quayam Educational and Welfare Trust.

Established as a premier co-educational residential and day-boarding institution, the Academy provides sound, modern, value-based education to boys and girls without prejudice to religion, caste, creed, or culture. Character formation and the inculcation of moral and ethical principles are our central commitments.

Our Foundational Philosophy

"School is nurturing the students under qualified teachers with day by day evaluation, and an effective focus on co-scholastic learning for the mental and physical development of every child."

Academic Framework

CBSE Curriculum & Pedagogical Structure

Structured learning pathways from Primary foundations through Class X Secondary Board examinations under standard CBSE subject combinations.

Primary School (Class I – V)

Strong literacy and numeracy foundations, interactive language development (English, Hindi, Urdu), environmental studies, and creative arts in supportive classrooms.

Focus: Cognitive Skills & Habit Formation

Middle School (Class VI – VIII)

Conceptual science experiments, advanced mathematics, social sciences, computer education with 40-terminal lab practice, and inter-house scholastic competitions.

Focus: Analytical Thinking & Science Labs

Secondary School (Class IX – X)

Rigorous preparation for CBSE All-India Secondary School Examination (AISSE). Subjects: 041 Mathematics, 086 Science, 087 Social Science, 184 English, 085 Hindi, and 303 Urdu.

Focus: 96.96% Board Track Record

Campus & Facilities

Purpose-Built Infrastructure on 2 Acres

Situated along NH-57 at Rampur Kodarkatti, the Academy campus spans 8,093 square meters comprising 3 building blocks and 33 rooms engineered for quiet, focused learning.

  • 20 Spacious Classrooms & Smart Class with natural ventilation and digital teaching aids.
  • 4 Advanced Science Labs & 40-PC Computer Lab for hands-on experimental mastery.
  • Resourceful Library (30×20 ft) stocking 3,000+ reference volumes, 18 periodicals, and 25 magazines.
  • Campus Health Clinic staffed with 5 medical beds, first-aid station, and periodic health checkups.
