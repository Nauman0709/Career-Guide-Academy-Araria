Student Life & Co-Curriculars

Beyond textbooks: Building resilience, leadership, and camaraderie through Bharat Scouts & Guides, inter-house debates, athletics, and cultural festivities.

National Recognition

Bharat Scouts & Guides Troop

Scouting at Career Guide Academy is not merely an extra-curricular hobby; it is a core vehicle for character formation, self-reliance, and national service.

Under dedicated troop leaders, our Scouts and Guides undertake rigorous training in pioneering, first aid, flag ceremonies, outdoor camping, and community disaster readiness.

Presidential Felicitation

The Career Guide Academy scout contingent achieved the rare institutional distinction of being formally received by the President of India, Pranab Mukherjee, reflecting the exemplary discipline and national standing of our cadets.

Traditions & Observances

Annual Celebrations & Scholastic Contests

Instilling pride in national heritage and fostering intellectual curiosity through state and district level competitions.

Maulana Abul Kalam Azad Day

Celebrated annually to honor India's first Education Minister with inter-school speech contests and state-level quizzes. Our students achieved 2nd Prize in the S.I.O Araria Quiz on this occasion.

Annual Toppers Award & Scholarship

Organized by Quayam Educational and Welfare Trust. Top performers in each class receive trophies, merit certificates, and financial scholarships. Teacher awards presented by Central University PR dignitaries.

State Level Quiz & Olympiads

Regular participation in State Level Quiz Contests organized by Navodaya Alumni of Bihar (securing 7th Rank in Senior Category), Private School Association contests, and NSTSE Hyderabad Olympiads.
