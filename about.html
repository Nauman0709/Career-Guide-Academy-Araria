About Career Guide Academy

Founded in 1996 in Araria, Bihar — rooted in timeless ethical values, modern academic inquiry, and selfless public service.

Genesis & Founding Story

Established in 1996 with an Unwavering Mission

The Career Guide Academy was founded in 1996 under the pioneering leadership of Prof. Raqueeb Ahmad, alongside esteemed colleagues Md. Iqubal (Alig), Abrar Ahmad, Syed Shamim Anwar, Shagufta Khanam, Moulana Mohd. Murtaza, and well-wishers dedicated to advancing youth education in Seemanchal, Bihar.

Recognizing the critical need for a sound modern educational institution in the region, the founders envisioned an academy that would offer high academic standards without prejudice to religion, caste, creed, or culture. Character formation and the inculcation of religious and moral principles remain the chief concern of school authorities, sparing no effort to foster lifelong habits of truth, honesty, and civic responsibility.

In November 2011, the sponsoring organization was formalized as Quayam Educational and Welfare Trust (Society Registration No. 167), establishing an enduring framework for institutional governance, scholarships, and educational development across rural and semi-urban communities.

Verified School Record: Academy students felicitated by academic dignitaries for outstanding performance in national competitions.

Our Institutional Motto

"Education as We Perceive"

Prominently emblazoned on our official crest, this motto signifies holistic perception — perceiving education not merely as bookish rote learning, but as a light illuminating character, discipline, intellect, and empathy.

Our Vision

To be an exemplary center of academic distinction and moral uprightness in Bihar, preparing young boys and girls to succeed in competitive examinations, contribute meaningfully to the nation, and uphold universal human dignity.

Our Mission

To deliver balanced CBSE education supported by well-equipped laboratories, a rich library, continuous assessment, co-curricular houses, and dedicated faculty, ensuring every student discovers their unique potential.

Institutional Pillars

Core Institutional Values

The guiding principles underpinning daily student life, teaching, and school administration.

Academic Integrity

Rigorous daily evaluation and conceptual understanding over superficial memorization.

Secular Inclusivity

Uncompromising equality for boys and girls of all communities, religions, and social strata.

Self-Discipline

Punctuality, respectful conduct, 85% mandatory attendance, and obedience to school regulations.

Holistic Welfare

Nurturing mind and body through active sports, arts, scouts, and student wellness support.
